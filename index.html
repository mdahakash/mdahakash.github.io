I am a CSE undergraduate at RUET (Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology), Bangladesh, with research interests in Computer Vision, Medical Image Analysis, Healthcare AI, Federated Learning, and Trustworthy & Explainable AI. I have authored IEEE conference papers in medical imaging, natural language processing, and satellite image segmentation. My long-term goal is to pursue a Ph.D. and develop trustworthy, privacy-preserving AI systems that address real-world challenges in healthcare.

My current research focuses on semi-supervised learning for retinal OCT image classification, where I develop efficient attention-based deep learning models to improve diagnostic performance while reducing the need for large-scale annotated medical datasets.

Published — 2nd International Conference on QPAIN, 2026.

An explainable DL framework for multi-class Alzheimer's disease diagnosis from structural MRI, combining a custom multi-scale CNN with transfer learning and ensemble techniques to improve classification robustness.

Md Ashraful Hossen Akash et al. (2025).
Memory-Efficient Deep Learning Architectures for Water Body Segmentation in Satellite Imagery: A Comparative Analysis and Ensemble Approach.
Published — 28th International Conference on Computer and Information Technology (ICCIT), 2025.

A comparative study of memory-efficient deep learning architectures for satellite-based water body segmentation, with an ensemble approach balancing accuracy and computational efficiency for resource-constrained environments.
