Prospective PhD Applicant: I am actively seeking PhD opportunities in Computer Science / BME / ECE for Fall 2027 / Spring 2028. My research interests span Medical Image Computing, Semi-Supervised Learning, and Trustworthy & Explainable AI.

I build data-efficient and interpretable deep learning systems that can diagnose pathologies reliably when expert medical annotations are sparse.

I graduated with a B.Sc. in Computer Science & Engineering (1st Class) from Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET), Bangladesh on August 11, 2026 (CGPA: 3.68 across last 61.75 credits). Currently, I conduct research as a Research Intern at Elite Research Lab LLC (New York, USA) focusing on multimodal architectures & federated learning, and as a Research Assistant at Arché Intelligence Lab working on 3D neuro-oncological MRI segmentation & grading.

My primary research centers on semi-supervised learning under distribution shifts (such as our TRIAGE framework under review at Scientific Reports), explainability & anatomical attribution for multi-site medical images (Chest X-Ray and Brain MRI), and memory-efficient model architectures designed for deployment on resource-constrained clinical workstations.

Medical Image Analysis
Risk-controlled pseudo-labeling, retinal OCT classification, and 3D volumetric MRI brain tumor segmentation with SwinUNETR.
Trustworthy & Explainable AI
Quantifying anatomical attribution and feature stability under cross-institution distribution shift in clinical AI models.
Efficient & Compact Deep Learning
Engineering parameter-efficient CNNs and frequency-aware Vision Transformers (RepViT) for low-power edge deployment.
Federated Learning in Healthcare
Multi-institutional collaborative learning frameworks that protect patient privacy without sacrificing model generalization.
