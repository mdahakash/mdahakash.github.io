CV

I am a CSE undergraduate at RUET (Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology), Bangladesh, with research interests in Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, and Natural Language Processing. I have authored IEEE conference papers focusing on transformer-based NLP, satellite image segmentation, and efficient deep learning models for medical imaging. My goal is to pursue advanced research and build impactful AI-driven solutions.

I am currently working on a federated semi-supervised learning framework for retinal OCT image classification, combining privacy-preserving distributed learning with state-of-the-art attention-based architectures.
